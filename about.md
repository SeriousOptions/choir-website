<div><p class="eyebrow text-teal">Who we are</p><h2>Adventurous by nature</h2></div>
<div class="prose"><p>Serious Options is known for performing adventurous and unusual repertoire in creative ways. Our music encompasses a great range of styles—from classical works and Broadway tunes with choreography to more avant-garde contemporary pieces.</p><p>The choir generally presents two programs each year, in late winter and late spring. Serious Options also works with choirs from around British Columbia, hosts joint concerts, travels to other communities, and participates in local choral showcases.</p><p>Our members come from all walks of life and include professional and amateur musicians of many ages, all sharing an enthusiasm for passionate and innovative performance.</p></div>
</div></section>

<section class="section section-warm" aria-labelledby="leadership-title"><div class="container"><div class="section-heading"><p class="eyebrow text-teal">Leadership</p><h2 id="leadership-title">The people behind the music</h2></div>
<div class="profile-grid">
<article class="profile-card profile-featured"><p class="role">Artistic Director</p><h3>Noralee Quast</h3><p>Noralee is the founder and artistic director of Serious Options, leading the choir since its beginning nearly 30 years ago. After involvement in high-calibre music programs on the coast, she moved to Kamloops to teach at the elementary level and complete a Master of Education.</p><p>Her career has included many school choirs and musicals, the Westsyde Boys’ Choir, Kamloops School District Children’s Choir, Kamloops Youth Choir, and Higher Ground. Members value her passionate and quirky directing style, caring nature, attention to detail, and commitment to outstanding choral repertoire.</p></article>
<article class="profile-card"><p class="role">Collaborative Pianist</p><h3>Lori Jane Froese</h3><p>Lori Jane joined Serious Options in 1997 and has collaboratively accompanied the choir ever since. In addition to her ARCT in piano, she holds degrees from the University of Calgary with specialties in flute and Kodály.</p><p>She is well known for imaginative classroom music programs and creative musicals throughout School District 73, where she also served as Music Coordinator.</p></article>
<article class="profile-card"><p class="role">Collaborative Pianist</p><h3>Ellen McMillan</h3><p>A born-and-raised Kamloopsian, Ellen received her music degree in piano and voice from UBC, where she later completed her education degree.</p><p>After teaching for more than 30 years in the Kamloops-Thompson district, she now enjoys singing and playing with Serious Options and directing her own women’s choir.</p></article>
</div></div></section>
